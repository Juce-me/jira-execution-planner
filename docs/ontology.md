# Project Ontology

Coverage: verified entry points and relationships for the Statistics area involved in the 2026-09-10 update and the ENG Story-readiness planning area reviewed on 2026-09-16. Other application areas remain unmapped.

## ENG Story Readiness

Verified on: 2026-09-17.

- **Catch Up hierarchy**: Story-oriented ENG list grouped under Epic headers and optionally Initiative headers. Entry points: `frontend/src/dashboard.jsx`, `frontend/src/eng/useEngWorkHierarchy.js`, `frontend/src/eng/engWorkHierarchy.js`, and `frontend/src/eng/EngView.jsx`; real Story row: `frontend/src/issues/IssueCard.jsx`; synthetic requirement row: `frontend/src/eng/StoryRequirementCard.jsx`; established layout tests: `tests/ui/codebase_structure_smoke.spec.js`, `tests/ui/eng_group_board_card.spec.js`, and `tests/ui/eng_missing_story_ghosts.spec.js`. Consumes visible selected-sprint Stories, normalized Epic metadata, and a complete Story-readiness snapshot; requirements remain outside real Story collections.
- **Planning hierarchy**: Catch Up's hierarchy with Planning controls, selection, Story Points, and capacity behavior. Entry points: `frontend/src/dashboard.jsx` and `frontend/src/eng/EngView.jsx`; tests: `tests/ui/planning_selection_defaults.spec.js` and `tests/ui/planning_capacity_editing.spec.js`. Depends on real Jira Story collections; synthetic requirements must not enter selection or capacity inputs.
- **Stories Required**: Catch-Up-only alert for active or future Story requirements. Alias: Needs Stories (legacy future-only name). Classification and target lookup come from `frontend/src/eng/engWorkHierarchy.js`; rendering: `frontend/src/eng/EngAlertsPanel.jsx`; local navigation: `frontend/src/eng/alertEpicNavigation.js`. It consumes the same complete readiness snapshot as the hierarchy, groups by the Team-catalog display name, counts unique Epics in the alert header, and clears target-hiding list state to neutral before its bounded local reveal retry. Verified 2026-09-16.
- **Alert Epic candidate**: normalized Epic record returned in `epicsInScope` for Catch Up alert classification. Producer: `fetch_epics_for_empty_alert` in `jira_server.py` and `build_alert_epic_payloads` in `backend/services/alert_epics.py`; consumer: `frontend/src/eng/useEngSprintData.js`. The alerts-purpose response adds story counts/distribution; the ordinary task response intentionally omits them to protect first-screen performance.
- **Alert-to-item navigation**: local reveal, filter clear, scroll, focus/edit, highlight, and Jira fallback for an alert row. Entry points: `frontend/src/eng/alertStoryNavigation.js`, `frontend/src/eng/EngAlertsPanel.jsx`, and `scrollToTaskItem`/`handleAlertStoryClick` in `frontend/src/dashboard.jsx`; coverage: `tests/test_dashboard_alert_source_guards.js` and `tests/ui/eng_alerts_panel_summary.spec.js`. It currently targets real Story DOM nodes and is not an Epic/Team target interface.
- **Story requirement**: application-owned representation of one unmet `(Department, selected Sprint, Epic, expected Team)` contribution. Alias: missing-story ghost. It is not a Jira issue and is produced only from a complete readiness snapshot. Projection: `frontend/src/eng/engWorkHierarchy.js`; rendering and external-link contract: `frontend/src/eng/StoryRequirementCard.jsx`; styles: `frontend/src/styles/eng/issues.css`; current source/style contract tests: `tests/test_eng_board_styles.js`. It depends on exact Team-label mappings, exhaustive child distribution, and normalized Initiative/Epic metadata; it produces a composite local alert target and never enters Story selection, capacity, statistics, dependencies, exports, or mutations.
- **Story-readiness snapshot**: exhaustive, scope-stamped read model for active/future Sprint coverage. Route: `GET /api/eng/story-readiness` in `backend/routes/eng_routes.py`; pure projection: `project_story_readiness` in `backend/services/story_readiness.py`; effective Department resolver: `load_effective_groups` in `backend/services/shared_group_config.py`; client lifecycle: `frontend/src/eng/useStoryReadiness.js`; contracts: `tests/test_story_readiness.py` and `tests/test_story_readiness_api.js`. It consumes authenticated Jira Epic/Story reads, exact Team labels, canonical sprint metadata, workspace configuration, and project-access state; it produces requirements only after exhaustive pagination and current-scope validation.

## Statistics

Verified on: 2026-09-10.

- **Statistics**: ENG dashboard reporting surface. Entry point: `frontend/src/dashboard.jsx`; API wrappers: `frontend/src/api/statsApi.js`; feature contract: `docs/features/statistics.md`; UI coverage: `tests/ui/codebase_structure_smoke.spec.js`.
- **Statistics source**: cached sprint-scoped Jira Story payload shared by Excluded Capacity, Mono vs Cross, and Project Track. Producer: `fetch_excluded_capacity_stats_source` and `build_excluded_capacity_issue_payload` in `jira_server.py`; route: `backend/routes/stats_routes.py`; contract tests: `tests/test_excluded_capacity_stats_api.py`. Produces normalized Story records with parent-Epic metadata consumed by Project Track.
- **Project Track**: Statistics view that groups Story Points by the parent Epic's Project Track value. Aliases in the UI: Project Track view, Epic mode, Team mode. Aggregation: `frontend/src/stats/projectTrackStats.js`; rendering: `frontend/src/stats/ProjectTrackTotalsBar.jsx`, `frontend/src/stats/ProjectTrackSprintChart.jsx`, `frontend/src/stats/ProjectTrackBreakdownChart.jsx`, and `frontend/src/stats/ProjectTrackPhaseChart.jsx`; tests: `tests/test_project_track_stats.js` and the Project Track case in `tests/ui/codebase_structure_smoke.spec.js`. Depends on the Statistics source and Jira issue-list links.
- **Lead Times**: Statistics view for Epic cohorts and elapsed time. Alias: Cohort Heatmap. Data model: `frontend/src/cohort/cohortUtils.js`; heatmap rendering: `frontend/src/cohort/CohortGrid.jsx`; styles: `frontend/src/styles/stats/cohort.css`; API route: `/api/stats/epic-cohort`; UI coverage: the Statistics and Lead Times cases in `tests/ui/codebase_structure_smoke.spec.js`.
- **Jira issue-list link**: external Jira search navigation for a filtered set of issues. Link analytics contract: `frontend/src/analytics/externalLinks.js`; tracked anchor: `frontend/src/components/TrackedExternalLink.jsx`; taxonomy: `docs/README_ANALYTICS.md`. Consumed by Statistics tables and Lead Times charts.
